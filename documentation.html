HELP & REFERENCE

VozKovo Documentation.

Everything you need to know about generating speech, choosing voices, adding pauses and keeping your audio files.

01 / GETTING STARTED

Turn text into speech

  1. Enter or paste your text in the editor.
  2. Search the voice catalogue and select the voice you want to use.
  3. Adjust the reading speed, then select Generate voice.
  4. Listen to the result and download the MP3 file.

VozKovo retrieves the available voices from its speech provider. You do not need to add an API key in the app.

02 / LONG TEXT

No fixed 5,000-character app cap

VozKovo does not impose a fixed 5,000-character limit or a fixed monthly character allowance in the application. Long text is automatically split into smaller sections, generated one after another and combined into a single MP3.

Important: this does not mean literally infinite generation. The speech provider, server resources, processing time, network connection and hosting plan can still impose practical limits or temporary availability restrictions.

03 / PAUSE MARKERS

Control pauses inside your text

Type a marker on its own or between sentences. VozKovo removes the marker from the spoken text and inserts actual silence in the audio. The markers work regardless of the selected website language or voice language.

[silence]Inserts a one-second pause. [silencio]Also inserts a one-second pause. [pause 1s]Inserts a one-second pause. [pausa 2s]Inserts a two-second pause. [break 500ms]Inserts a half-second pause.

Supported units include milliseconds (ms) and seconds (s, sec, seconds, seg and segundos). A pause without a duration defaults to one second. Each marker is capped at ten seconds.

04 / YOUR BROWSER LIBRARY

Your generated MP3 files stay on your device

After generation, VozKovo automatically saves a copy of the MP3 in this browser's local database. You can play, download or delete saved files from the Saved audio in this browser section.

The voice catalogue and your last selected voice are also cached in the browser to make returning to the app quicker. Browser storage is device- and browser-specific; clearing site data or using another browser/device may remove or hide these files.

The server keeps a temporary copy while the job is being prepared. The browser library is a separate local copy and does not sync between devices.

05 / LANGUAGES & VOICES

Website language is independent from speech language

The interface can follow your browser language or be changed manually. Choosing English for the website does not force the generated speech to use an English voice. Select the voice that matches the language you want to hear.

Service availability

VozKovo uses the online the speech provider speech service through the the configured speech provider package. Internet access is required on the server. Voice availability and generation speed may change when the external service or hosting environment changes.
